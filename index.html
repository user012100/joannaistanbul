Joanna is a future classic restaurant and bar located in a three-floor building in Arnavutköy, Beşiktaş. More than a venue, it’s

a layered experience. With four distinct levels, Joanna blends dining, music, study, and bar culture into a rhythm of its own — from

daytime rituals to nighttime release, from stillness to movement, from sunlight to club dark.

 

On the ground floor, a coffee bar greets the street; a seaside restaurant and open kitchen set the tone for shared moments

and plates. Upstairs, the first floor — The Living Room — is quieter, slower. Offers a space to study, collective workshops, or quiet

gatherings. Then comes the second floor, where the pulse changes. DJ sets echo, bodies move, senses blur. At the very top: The

Terrace. Cocktails, live sets, and the Bosphorus — with the sky as the ceiling.

 

She is intentionally multifaceted, holds contrast with ease. The nobility in her blood, and the sweat of 2nd floor on her skin.

But it’s never complete without you. Every visit shapes her — calm or wild, quiet or loud. She invites you to find her for yourself.
